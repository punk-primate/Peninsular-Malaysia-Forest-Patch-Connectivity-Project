Forests in a changing landscape

This research-based website brings together information about forest patches and potential connections for arboreal wildlife in Peninsular Malaysia. More cities will be added in future updates.

Open a landscape, zoom in and select a forest patch to see its area, structural characteristics and modelled connectivity information. The map also includes a development tool for exploring how a proposed road or site could affect forest and potential routes.

The maps support exploration and discussion. Potential connections represent modelled opportunities for movement, which need field assessment.
