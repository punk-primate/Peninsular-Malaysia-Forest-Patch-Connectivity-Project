Peninsular Malaysia Forest Patch Connectivity Project

This interactive map visualises forest patches in Kuantan region of Malaysia. Each patch is classified into one of six tiers using a Composite Habitat Suitability Index (CHSI), which combines landscape metrics including total area, core area, contiguity, perimeter-area ratio, and distance to the nearest neighbouring patch. Connectivity values are derived from circuit theory modelling using Omniscape. Click on any patch to view its details.

How to Use This Map

Navigating the map

Zoom in until forest patches appear. Click any patch to see its details in the sidebar. Use the search bar to find specific locations.

What the patch colours mean

Patches are coloured by conservation tier, from light green (Tier 1, highest structural quality) to dark green (Tier 6, highly isolated micro-patches). Use the Filter by Category checkboxes to show or hide specific tiers.

Potential movement corridors

Click Show Corridors to reveal lines connecting forest patches to their nearest neighbours. The High, Moderate, and Low buttons toggle visibility by connectivity level — a checkmark shows which levels are currently displayed. Click any corridor line to see details in the sidebar.

Reading patch details

Clicking a patch shows its conservation tier, connectivity rating, and distance to the nearest adjacent patch. Expand Show technical details for the full set of landscape metrics.
