Peninsular Malaysia Forest Patch Connectivity Project

This interactive map visualises forest patches in Kuantan region of Malaysia. Each patch is classified into one of six tiers using a Composite Habitat Suitability Index (CHSI), which combines landscape metrics including total area, core area, contiguity, perimeter-area ratio, and distance to the nearest neighbouring patch. Connectivity values are derived from circuit theory modelling using Omniscape. Click on any patch to view its details.

How to use this map

Getting started

Zoom into any part of Kuantan until green forest patches appear on the map. You can also use the search bar in the sidebar to jump to a specific location. The map works best on a desktop browser.

What the patch colours mean

Each patch is coloured according to its conservation tier, which reflects its structural quality as a habitat:

  • Tier 1 (Core habitat) - the most ecologically intact patches, large enough to support a high level of biodiversity with substantial buffered interior area
  • Tier 2 (Major stepping stones) - high-quality patches that act as critical hubs or potential bridges in a movement network
  • Tier 3 (Connected fragments) - patches with moderate connectivity potential to help link larger areas of forest
  • Tier 4 (Vulnerable edge fragments) - smaller patches with significant edge exposure that are still functionally important
  • Tier 5 (Isolated fragments) - small patches with limited connectivity potential to the wider network
  • Tier 6 (Isolated micro patches) - highly isolated remnant fragments, generally too small to support resident populations

Use the "Filter by category" checkboxes in the sidebar to show or hide individual tiers on the map.

Clicking on a patch

Click any forest patch to see its details in the sidebar. The panel shows the conservation tier, a description of what it means ecologically, the connectivity potential rating, and the distance to the nearest adjacent patch. Expand "Show technical details" to see the individual landscape metrics. Each metric has a small "i" button you can click for an explanation.

Connectivity potential rating

The connectivity potential rating is derived from Omniscape circuit theory modelling, which simulates how movement current flows across the landscape. High indicates the patch sits in a potential corridor zone; Moderate indicates partial connectivity potential; Low indicates high landscape resistance and low connectivity potential; Barrier indicates the patch is surrounded by effectively impermeable matrix with no connectivity potential.

Potential movement corridors

Click "Show corridors" to reveal lines connecting forest patches to their nearest neighbours. Each line is categorised by their level of connectivity potential. Use the High, Moderate, and Low buttons to toggle each level on or off. Click any corridor line to see details in the sidebar.

Filtering patches

Use the "Filter by patch area" tool in the sidebar to show only patches above or below a certain size in hectares. This can help you focus on large core habitats or identify which smaller patches are most at risk.
